Design system v1 · 9 Oct 2026

One system for the page and the app. About a fifth lighter, a lot calmer.

An audit of the newest landing (branch pricing-frontend: pricing v3 and the 80% column) and every dashboard screen, a study of 20 best-in-class AI and SaaS sites, and the design system and brand kit that came out of it. Research and design only: nothing in the product changed. The tokens are ready for the build step on staging.

The direction in five lines

Graphite, paper and one mint. Evolved, not replaced.

The brand stays: Geist, the Stack Fan, mint, the instrument panel. What changes is how dark, how loud and how many ways the same thing is drawn.

  1. 01

    Graphite, not black. The dark ground moves from #050505 to a mint-tinted graphite ladder: #0E110F deep, #141715 page, #1B1F1C card, #232825 raised, #2C322E selected. Cards separate from the page by surface step, not by glow.

  2. 02

    Paper where people read and decide. Winning patterns and the pricing zone sit on warm paper #F6F5F0 with bone #EDECE5 and white cards: about a quarter of the scroll, bracketed by graphite. That is the "20% lighter" in practice.

  3. 03

    Mint means act or proof, nothing else. One primary button, live dots, progress and proof numbers. Headlines go two-tone in grey, not mint, so the button is always the brightest thing on screen. No glows, no gold stars.

  4. 04

    One type voice. Geist 550 for display (the wordmark's own weight) at 64 and 44px, Geist 400 for text at 16 to 19px, Geist Mono 500 for 12px labels and data. The H1 is the biggest thing on the page again, and nothing ships under 12px.

  5. 05

    One frame for everything. The 80% column, an 8pt scale, six radii, 1px hairlines, opacity and transform motion only, and one ad frame (4:5, 10px radius, mono caption) used on the page and in the app.

01 · Audit

Good parts, too many dialects. Here is what doesn't add up.

Every number below was read from the computed CSS of each element on the running branch (localhost, vite), at 1440 and 390, plus 21 dashboard screens and states in mock mode.

7CTA labels for 3 intents (trial, plans, Free) across about 16 CTAs
15,125px of page at 1440 (live: 13,424)
23,050px at 390: 27 phone screens; pricing alone is 3,650

The 10 biggest findings

01

The heading hierarchy is upside down.

The hero H1 is 50px while section H2s run 56px (six sections), 48px (three), 60px (Guarantee, final CTA) and 36px (BuildLine). Five sizes do one job, and alignment flips between left (7) and centred (5). Each section reads like it came from a different page, which is exactly the "not one landing page" feeling.

02

Too dark, and the same dark for 15,000px.

Everything sits on #050505 (L* 1.4), darker than Linear #08090A or Retool #151515. Panels are #0A0B0A to #0E100E, so cards barely lift off the page and need glows and crop ticks to be seen. There is no light section, so the scroll is one long black slab.

03

Two visual languages on one page.

The newer instrument panel kit (weekly/ui.tsx: mono labels, hairlines, square tags) sits next to older pieces: the spinning seal with a green glow, gradient gold stars, radial glows in the hero and final CTA, pill toggles, a niche marquee, and an Ads Manager clone in Meta blue and the system font.

04

Mint is everywhere, so it stops meaning "click here".

11 of the 13 headlines carry a mint clause, plus mint eyebrow dots, tags, progress, links, checkmarks, prices per ad and three button styles. When the accent is everywhere the CTA is never the brightest thing on screen.

05

CTA sprawl.

About 16 CTAs carry 7 labels for 3 intents: four for the trial (Start free trial, Get my 5 free ads, Get my ads, Start 7-day free trial), one for plans (See the plans), two for Free (Watch my competitors, Start on Free). Corners of 7 and 9px, with and without a glow. The hero's "See the plans" is an orphan link, left-aligned under centred microcopy.

06

The story is told twice, and the page is long.

The hero ends with the 5-step BuildLine ("From your link to Monday's drop"), then DropLoop explains the same build again ("Your competitors test"). BuildLine also runs 1,100px wide while every section below uses the 880px column. On a phone the page is 27 screens.

07

Text is too small and too dim to scan.

The landing sets about 100 elements at 10px, mostly mono labels, plus a 9px seal label. In the dashboard the most common sizes are 9.5 to 12.5px, and text at white 0.32 and 0.42 fails AA (2.7:1 and 4.0:1). Page titles are 15px under 27px card headings.

08

Pricing is crowded at the decision point.

Four plans, a billing toggle and an anchor line, then the same trial microcopy four times, a 6-tile add-on grid with two "Coming soon" tiles, a 5+ stores band and a compare accordion: 1,800px at desktop. Prices use weight 900, which only the seal shares.

09

The first trust signal is decoration.

With no customer logos yet (correctly: none are faked), the first thing under the form is a row of gold stars on USPs that read like ratings, and an uppercase niche marquee. The real proof, a dated Meta Ad Library board with days live, starts 4,000px down.

10

The app and the page look like two brands.

Landing: #050505 with Geist Mono labels. Dashboard: #111 cards, blue-tinted leftovers from the indigo era (rgb 11,11,18 and 14,14,20), no Geist Mono at all, buttons at 8, 10, 14, 16, 20px and pill. And the page shows square 1:1 example ads while every plan ships 4:5.

Mint rules

  • Fills: only the primary button (500, ink-0 label, 10.7:1), progress bars, live dots, the selected toggle dot.
  • Text on dark: 500 for proof numbers and links (10.2:1 on ink-1). Text on paper: 800 #1D7047 (5.6:1); never 500 on paper (1.6:1).
  • On paper the mint button carries a 1px 700 edge so its boundary passes 3:1.
  • Tints: 950 on dark and 50 on paper mark the recommended card, a selected row, a success well.
  • Never: mint headlines, mint glows, mint gradients outside the logo, mint on a mint-tinted card.

Secondary accent and semantics

No second brand accent. The ads supply the colour. Semantic colours exist for state only, each with a dark and a light value:

Success #71D793 / #1D7047 Warning #E8B04B / #8A5A00 Danger #F28B82 / #B42318 Info #8AB8F5 / #1D5FB4

Rating stars, if real ratings ever appear, use a solid #E8B04B. Not the gradient gold, and never on USPs.

When a section goes light, and how it pairs

Paper

For reading and deciding: pattern notes, pricing, the risk strip. White cards with a 1px l2 line and shadow l1; one dark card allowed on paper (the recommended plan).

Bone

Only inside a paper zone: wells, quota boxes, the risk strip, or a second paper section in the same act. Never next to dark directly.

Pairing

Hard cut, no gradient between tones, 128px of padding on both sides. A paper zone is always bracketed by graphite. Never two light zones in a row, never more than three dark sections in a row.

Spacing, grid, radii, elevation

8pt scale with a 4px half step

Plus 96, 128, 160 for sections. Section padding: 128 when a section opens an act, 96 when it continues one (mobile 80 and 64). Head to content 48 (32). Card padding 24 (20). Between blocks 32 (24).

Grid and the 80% column

Page column 1180px with 40px sides. Every section below the hero keeps the owner's 80% column: 880px of content at desktop, 16px gutters on phones. Inside it, 12 columns with 24px gutters. Heads split 7/5; galleries 3 or 4 up; pricing 4 up at 880 (2 up under 1000px, 1 up on phones). Text measure 640px. Only the hero may use the full 1100px stage.

7 / 5 split head: title left, lead right, bottoms aligned.

Six radii, replacing 17 across page and app

Nested corners: inner radius = outer minus padding (a 16px card with 8px padding holds 8px corners).

Elevation and borders

e0 · ink-1 flat
e1 dark · ink-2, line-2, top highlight
e1 light · white, l1, shadow-l1
e2 light · floating product shot

On dark, depth comes from the surface step, not a shadow. Every border is 1px. No glows, no blur behind cards, no backdrop-filter on full-width layers.

Icons, motion, imagery

Icons

Lucide only (already in the repo), 1.5px stroke, 16px in text and buttons, 20px in cards, currentColor. Icons sit in text colour; mint only on a success check. No emoji, no gradient icons, no icon tiles with glows. Steps use mono numbers (01, 02) instead of icons.

Motion

Opacity and transform only. Hover 120ms, press and toggles 200ms, accordion 320ms, reveal 480ms with a 12px rise, once, max 6 staggered at 60ms. No continuous animation on the page: no marquee, no spinning seal, no drifting backdrop. One live-dot pulse inside a status pill is the exception. Everything off screen is paused; reduced motion turns it all off.

Ads and imagery

The ad frame: 4:5 (1:1 only where the source is square), 10px radius, 1px inner hairline, mono caption below. On paper an ad sits on a white mat with 8px padding. Tilt only in the hero stack, max 6 degrees, three cards, echoing the Stack Fan. No device frames except for native-interface ads, no stock people at laptops.

04 · Typography

Keep Geist. Use it like Vercel does, not like a poster.

Geist is the wordmark, it's on Google Fonts, and it's already loaded. The fix is weight, scale and discipline, not a new family.

Display · Geist 550

The wordmark's own weight. Down from 700: on screen at 44 to 64px, 550 reads confident and calm, the way Linear (510) and Vercel (400 to 450) set theirs.

Text · Geist 400 and 500

Body 16px at 1.6, leads 19px. 500 for UI labels, 600 for card titles and buttons. 900 retires (prices go to 600).

Mono · Geist Mono 500

Labels, eyebrows and data at 12px, tracked +0.08em, uppercase only for labels. Tabular numerals for prices and counts in sans. Added to the dashboard, which has none today.

Considered and rejected: a serif display pairing (Harvey, Granola, Resend, Mintlify do it well). It signals editorial or legal luxury, adds a font request, and our ads already bring the visual variety. Geist only, two families, 400 to 700.

Clear space and minimum size

Clear space on all sides: 0.25 times the mark's height. Nothing else enters it, including the page edge.

Minimums: full mark 48px and up; compact cut 24 to 48px; two-card xs cut under 24px (16px floor). Horizontal lockup 20px tall, stacked lockup 48px tall.

Which file

  • Graphite and the app: ws-mark-dark, ws-lockup-horizontal-dark
  • Paper, invoices, print: ws-mark-light, ws-lockup-horizontal-light
  • On a mint fill or any mid green: ws-mark-flat-charcoal or flat-white
  • One colour, embroidery, fax: ws-mark-flat-*
  • Favicon: favicon.svg (switches with the OS theme)
