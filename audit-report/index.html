What was fixed

AreaFixed
CriticalPayment test mode could open real trials on production. Closed the same hour.
Selling
  • Retired packs, the old Test plan and add-ons can no longer be bought.
  • Only the 4 plans sell.
  • The bot check is on every checkout made without a login.
  • Exact per-IP limits, plus a Cloudflare limit on the payment pages.
Abuse
  • One trial per store, email and card, and every trial drop is reviewed.
  • Open sign-up is closed.
  • Competitor research is for trial and paid accounts only, with a daily cap.
  • Trial accounts have a spend cap, and generation stops if the cost counter can't be read.
  • Alerts for failed payment spikes and for cost.
Ad engine
  • The product's own label passes, while made-up claims still fail.
  • Wrapped and slightly reworded lines pass.
  • Empty boxes fail, with a hard backup check.
  • Failed product reads retry, alert you once and email the customer.
  • Early drops wait for the product instead of failing.
  • The real reason is shown when a plan's ads are used up.
Billing and emails
  • Grace starts from the failure.
  • New emails for a failed payment and a downgrade.
  • Real ad counts and first names, and the bonus shown separately.
  • Old pack emails never reach plan customers.
Landing
  • Terms, refund and privacy rewritten for the trial and subscription.
  • A true guarantee seal ("New ads every Monday, flawed ads rebuilt free").
  • A new share image and one rebuild promise everywhere.
  • The ad check promise and the "4 bonuses" line are hidden until real.
  • A branded 404.
App
  • Plan names instead of "Test Pack".
  • A clear "In review" state.
  • Billing shows the real price and next charge, with cancel and resume in the app.
  • Pack-era add-ons and bonuses are hidden for plan customers.
  • "Download everything" works.
  • Email links land on the drop.

Speed cuts for your yes or no

1 Auto-release first drops that pass every check

Today
Every first drop waits for your manual review, which is the longest wait for a new customer.
Change
Ads that pass the text check and the image check go straight to the customer. You review only the flagged ones, and a trial from a reused card or email is always held.
Gain
First ads in about 30 to 45 minutes instead of whenever you are free.
Risk
An occasional weak ad reaches a customer. The checks are much stronger after this audit.

2 No 30 minute research wait on the first drop

Change
The first drop uses the library right away; competitor patterns join from week 2.
Gain
Up to 30 minutes faster first ads. One setting.

3 Plain extension for 4:5, paid extension only for 9:16

Gain
About a minute and a little cost per ad. Most 4:5 extensions ended up as plain bands anyway.

4 Different store photos across weeks

Why
Scale reused one model photo for the whole month and felt monotonous. Rotate through the store's photos and prefer a clean pack shot as the hero.

5 Remove the pack-era code paths

Why
Fewer moving parts and fewer old emails or screens leaking through. No customer-visible change. Later, after launch feedback.
