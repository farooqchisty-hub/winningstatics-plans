Audit, month simulation and hardening plan

Use the product like a real customer, simulate a full month on every plan with weeks compressed to 2 minutes, check every ad, break the flows on purpose, then fix what matters and cut what slows the customer down.

In one screen

  • Free work starts now: the read-only click test, the consistency check and the attack review run on live and staging.
  • Paid work waits for your go: the month simulation runs on staging only, with 4 plans, 2 ads per drop and a week every 2 minutes. About $10 to $12, hard stop at $45 total (the pilot cap is $35 and $30.14 is spent).
  • Fixes: breakages and flow bugs get fixed and shipped as found, each with a rollback. New dashboard screens are proposed with mockups first, and you pick.
  • Output: one report page with a findings table (severity, evidence, fix, status), every ad sheet and the screenshots.

Phases

P0 Simulation harness staging only $0

Build
A staging-only time switch, so a week is 2 minutes and a trial is 2 minutes. It refuses to run on production. A per-drop cap of 2 ads for the simulation. A Dodo webhook simulator (signed events for trial start, charge, failed charge, cancel, resume, upgrade). 4 test accounts on staging, one per plan, with real stores.
Why
Lets a whole month run in about 15 minutes per plan without cards, without real Dodo charges and without touching production.
Time
About 1 hour.

P1 Real-user click test live + staging $0

Flows
  • New visitor on phone and desktop: landing, then "Get my 5 free ads", then /plans, then the checkout page (stop there, no card).
  • Arriving from a pricing button.
  • A returning store whose trial is used.
  • Bad inputs.
  • Back and refresh mid-flow.
  • Login (password test account).
  • Every dashboard screen in every state: trialing, active, cancelled, payment failed, out of ads.
  • Every email rendered.
  • /old/ and the legal pages.
Consistency
One matrix of prices, ads a month, videos, products, bonus, trial terms and timing promises across the landing, /plans, FAQ, Dodo product descriptions, checkout, emails, terms and dashboard. Every mismatch is a finding.
Health
Console errors, failed requests, 404s, dead links, page speed on phone, sideways scroll, broken images.
Time
About 1.5 hours.

P2 One month on every plan needs your go about $10 to $12

Run
4 accounts: Starter, Growth, Scale and Studio. Each goes through:
  • a trial drop of 2 ads, approved through the real review queue
  • the day-7 charge, with the bonus applied
  • 4 weekly drops 2 minutes apart, 2 ads each
Lifecycle twists
  • Starter cancels inside the trial: no charge, keeps the ads, no more drops.
  • Growth cancels mid-month and then resumes.
  • Scale's renewal payment fails: drops pause, then recover after payment.
  • Studio presses "Start my plan now", then downgrades to Scale.
Checked
  • Every ad, by eye, on contact sheets.
  • Every email, its timing and its wording.
  • Every dashboard state, with screenshots.
  • Ledger math: granted vs used vs left.
  • Drop timing, the bonus counted once, nothing after cancel, and no double charges or double grants on repeated webhooks.
Time
About 2 to 3 hours, mostly waiting on renders.

P3 Abuse, spam and attack review $0

Surfaces
  • /trial/lead and /checkout spam (Turnstile, per-IP and daily caps).
  • Card testing through checkout.
  • Trial farming: new domain, same card or email.
  • Cost attacks that burn image spend (circuit breakers, daily caps).
  • Webhook forgery and replay.
  • Admin key exposure.
  • Database rules: what the public key can read or write.
  • Storage listing.
  • Store URL reads used to reach internal hosts.
  • Prompt injection planted on a store page.
  • Email bombing through our emails.
  • Open redirects on checkout return.
  • Secrets in the frontend bundles.
  • Cloudflare rate limits and bot rules on the API and the landing.
Method
Read the code and the Cloudflare settings. Light single-request probes on production. Anything heavier than a handful of requests runs on staging only; there is no load test against production.
Time
About 1.5 hours, in parallel with P1.

P4 Cut what slows the customer proposals

Candidates
  • First drop: auto-release ads that pass every check, with your review only for flagged ones.
  • Drop the 30 min research wait on the first drop and add the competitor patterns from week 2.
  • Skip paid outpaint where plain bands are used anyway.
  • Retire dead switches and legacy pack paths the new product never uses.
  • Fewer steps between payment and the first ad.
Rule
Each one gets: time saved for the customer, cost saved, risk, and a yes/no for you.

P5 Fix and ship as found

Breakages
Fixed, tested and shipped right away, with a rollback each. Copy or consistency mismatches are fixed at the source.
Dashboard
Likely screens: Drops (this week, next Monday countdown, history), Competitor board (the report the landing sells), Plan and usage (ads left, products), Ad detail (3 sizes, Meta copy, download, rebuild), and first-drop progress. Mockups first; you pick; then build.

P6 Report

Page
Findings table, the ad sheets per plan, screenshots per screen and state, attack review results, the P4 proposals for your yes/no, and what shipped.

Guardrails

  • Anything paid runs on staging. Production gets read-only checks and single-request probes only.
  • No cards entered anywhere. No emails to real addresses. No business-wide Dodo settings.
  • The time switch only exists on staging and refuses production.
  • Every production deploy records its rollback first.
