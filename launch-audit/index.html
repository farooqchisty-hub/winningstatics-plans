Known small items left

  • Under a burst of 50 signups the first drop is released about 2 minutes later than at rest (queue waits for the store read and planning). Correct, just slower.
  • A webhook cut at exactly the wrong moment is recovered by the 10 minute sweep rather than by Dodo's retry.
  • Meta copy angle labels are cut at 24 characters; the first-drop email names one style for a mixed drop.
  • Smart Placement has not moved the Worker yet; the speed gain so far comes from parallel reads.
